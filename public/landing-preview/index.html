Before you sign up.

Contact DuQana support
Can I record cash sales without setting up M-Pesa?

Yes. Choose cash at checkout. Connect M-Pesa separately if you want to send payment prompts.

Can my cashier have their own login?

Yes. Each staff member can have their own login. The owner chooses which tasks they can access.

What do I need to get started?

Have your shop details, product names, prices and opening stock quantities ready. Read the setup guide.

Does a customer need an account to verify a receipt?

The receipt QR code opens a public verification page that customers can view without signing in.
